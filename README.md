## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🔒 TLS_NetworkEncryption

Demonstrates three stages of network security side by side: plain unencrypted TCP, TLS with server-side authentication, and mutual TLS (mTLS) with authentication on both ends - a practical look at the trust and encryption layers behind everyday HTTPS traffic.

### ☀️ Performance Optimization of a Photovoltaic System - Diploma Thesis

**Diploma thesis project - Team Lead, responsible for coordinating development from initial planning and system design through implementation & documentation.** Developed an intelligent energy management system that optimizes photovoltaic energy distribution among a home battery, EV charger, and hot water boiler to maximize solar self-consumption, reduce energy costs, and minimize grid dependency. Coordinated the development team while contributing to both frontend and backend implementation, using IoT devices, Raspberry Pi, and a React/Expo mobile app for real-time and historical monitoring.
